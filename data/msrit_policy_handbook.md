# Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT) — Revised Regulations for B.E. / B.Arch. Degree Programmes (2023-24 & 2024-25)

**Institution:** Ramaiah Institute of Technology (Autonomous Institute, Affiliated to VTU), Bangalore[cite: 12]
**Accreditation:** Approved by AICTE, New Delhi & Govt. of Karnataka; Accredited by NBA & NAAC with 'A+' Grade[cite: 12, 24]
**Note:** The regulations hereunder are subject to amendments as may be made by the VTU / Academic Council of the College from time to time[cite: 12].

---

## 1. Preliminary Definitions and Nomenclatures

* **Clause 1.1 (Autonomous Institute / College):** An institute/college designated as an autonomous institute/college by the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), as per the VTU Autonomous College Statutes, 2006 amended 2018[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.2 (Academic Autonomy):** Freedom to a College in all aspects of conducting its academic programmes, granted by the University for promoting excellence[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.3 (Commission):** University Grants Commission (UGC)[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.4 (AICTE):** All India Council for Technical Education[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.5 (CoA):** Council of Architecture[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.6 (University):** Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.7 (Institute):** Ramaiah Institute of Technology (formerly known as M. S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology), Bangalore[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.8 (Programme):** An educational program in a particular stream/branch of Engineering or Architecture leading to the award of a Degree (B.E., B.Arch., M.Tech., M.Arch., MBA, MCA, M.Sc. Engineering by Research, Ph.D.)[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.9 (Branch):** Specialization in a programme such as Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, etc.[cite: 13]
* **Clause 1.10 (Semester):** One of the two sessions (odd and even) of an academic year[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.11 (Academic Year):** Two consecutive semesters (odd followed by an even) sessions, including intervening periods[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.12 (Course):** A theory or practical subject identified by its course code and course title, normally studied in a semester[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.13 (Credit):** A unit or weightage by which coursework is measured, representing the number of hours of instruction prescribed per week[cite: 13]. One credit equals 1 hour of lecture or 2 hours of laboratory/practical/tutorials/fieldwork per week[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.14 (Continuous Internal Evaluation - CIE):** Formative assessment of students' performance during the teaching-learning process conducted by the course faculty through tests, assignments, group discussions, quizzes, projects, seminars, etc.[cite: 13]
* **Clause 1.15 (Semester End Examinations Evaluation - SEE):** Summative assessment examinations conducted at the end of the semester covering the entire syllabus[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.16 (Honours):** A higher recognition awarded to a well-performing UG student on earning specific extra credits after satisfying B.E. Degree award conditions in the same branch[cite: 13].
* **Clause 1.17 (Minor Degree):** Awarded along with a B.E. Degree if a student completes the required number of credits of another program[cite: 13].

## 3. Duration of the Programmes

* **Clause 3.1.1 (B.E. Normal Duration):** Four academic years leading to the Degree of Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) of VTU[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.1.2 (Lateral Entry B.E. Normal Duration):** Three academic years leading to the B.E. degree[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.1.3 (B.Arch. Normal Duration):** Five academic years leading to the Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) degree[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.2.1 (Maximum Duration - Regular):** 8 years for B.E. and 10 years for B.Arch.[cite: 14] Students failing to complete within this period must withdraw from the programme, though they may seek readmission to the first year as a fresh candidate[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.2.2 (Maximum Duration - Lateral Entry):** 6 years for B.E. lateral entry students[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.2.3 (Reckoning Period):** Calculated from the academic year in which the student is admitted for the first time into the degree programme[cite: 14].
* **Clause 3.3.1 – 3.3.4 (Semester Structure):** The academic year is divided into two main semesters of 19 weeks duration each, with a minimum of 90 working days per semester, following the Academic Calendar declared at the start of the semester[cite: 14]. *(Note: Updated to 20 weeks plus a 10-week Summer Semester in the 2025 VTU Addendum below)[cite: 24].*

## 4. Admissions

* **Clause 4.1 (General Admission):** Candidates must satisfy admission conditions prescribed by VTU, Government of Karnataka, CoA, and AICTE[cite: 14].
* **Clause 4.2 (Lateral Entry Admission):** Direct admission to Second Year B.E. is possible for candidates with a 3-year Diploma in Engineering/Polytechnic or B.Sc. qualification[cite: 14].

## 6. Credit System

* **Clause 6.1 (Credit-Based System):** Programmes follow a Credit System where students earn credits by satisfactorily completing courses every semester[cite: 15].
* **Clause 6.2 (Credit Definition):** Lecture (L) = 1 hour/week per credit; Tutorial (T) = 2 hours/week per credit; Practical (P) = 2 hours/week per credit[cite: 15].
* **Clause 6.3 – 6.4 (Course Registration & L:T:P Allocation):** Students must register before the beginning of every semester[cite: 15]. Example: 2 hrs Lecture + 2 hrs Tutorial + 2 hrs Practical = 2:1:1 (4 credits)[cite: 15].
* **Clause 6.5 (Grade Card):** Earned credits and grades are recorded on the semester Grade Card[cite: 15].
* **Clause 6.6 (Minimum Credits & CGPA for Degree Award):**
  * **B.E. (4 Years / 8 Semesters):** 160 credits[cite: 15].
  * **B.E. Lateral Entry (3 Years / 6 Semesters):** 120 credits[cite: 15].
  * **B.Arch. (5 Years / 10 Semesters):** 260 credits (as per CoA)[cite: 15].
  * **Minimum CGPA:** 5.0[cite: 15] *(Note: Revised to >= 4.00 for UG in the 2025 VTU Addendum)[cite: 26].*
  * **Honours / Minor:** Awarded upon earning a minimum of 18 additional credits[cite: 15].

## 7. Registration of Courses

* **Clause 7.1 (Credit Limits per Semester):** Students must register on time within a permissible weekly contact limit of ~35 hours/week[cite: 15]. B.E. students normally register for **18–24 credits** per semester (plus 1/2 units of NCMC) and **shall not exceed 28 credits** (including Minor/Honours, regular, and backlog courses)[cite: 16]. B.Arch. students register for **20–30 credits** (average 25)[cite: 16]. *(Note: Minimum credit floor updated to 16 credits in the 2025 VTU Addendum)[cite: 25].*
* **Clause 7.2 (Debarment from Registration):** Students may be barred from registering due to disciplinary reasons, non-payment of fees, etc.[cite: 16]
* **Clause 7.3 (Non-Credit Mandatory Courses):** Formal registration is mandatory at the start of the semester[cite: 16]. Failing attendance or minimum requirements results in a fail grade requiring re-registration in subsequent semesters[cite: 16].

## 8. Assessment and Grading

* **Clause 8.1 (CIE & SEE Weightage):** Assessment comprises **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)** and **Semester End Examination (SEE)** with **50% weightage each**[cite: 16]. Students must maintain a minimum standard in CIE to be permitted to appear in SEE[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.2 – 8.3 (Grading Scale & Grade Points):** Assigned via Relative or Absolute Grading[cite: 16]:
  * **O (Outstanding):** 90–100% | 10 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **A+ (Excellent):** 80–89% | 9 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **A (Very Good):** 70–79% | 8 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **B+ (Good):** 60–69% | 7 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **B (Above Average):** 55–59% | 6 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **C (Average):** 50–54% | 5 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **P (Pass):** 40–49% | 4 Grade Points[cite: 16]
  * **F (Fail):** 0–39% | 0 Grade Points[cite: 16]
* **Clause 8.4 (CIE Components):** Conducted throughout the semester by course faculty via mid-term tests, weekly/fortnightly class tests, assignments, problem-solving exercises, group discussions, quizzes, seminars, mini-projects, and surprise tests[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.5 (Internship / Seminar Evaluation):** Evaluated purely via CIE; there is no SEE[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.6 (SEE Conduct):** Written examination for theory courses and practical/design examination with built-in viva for lab/design courses[cite: 16]. Requires minimum attendance and CIE eligibility[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.7 (Backlog Exam Clashes):** Students with backlog courses may have two exams on the same day (morning and afternoon sessions) or consecutive days[cite: 16]. Examination timetables will not be altered; students must choose which exam to write per their Proctor's advice[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.8 – 8.9 (Credit Points Calculation):** Total course points = Course Credits × Grade Points earned[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.10 – 8.11.1 (Transitional Grade "I"):** Awarded when a student has satisfactory attendance and passing CIE, but remained absent from SEE for valid, convincing reasons (personal accident, serious illness, or death of parent/guardian) supported by official documents[cite: 16]. Permits the student to write a **Make-Up SEE**[cite: 16].
* **Clause 8.11.2 (Transitional Grade "X"):** Awarded if a student has **attendance >= 85%**, **CIE >= 90%**, attended the SEE, but performed poorly in SEE such that they would receive an overall "F" grade[cite: 17]. Permits the student to write a **Make-Up SEE**[cite: 17]. *Not applicable to practical subjects[cite: 17].*
* **Clause 8.12 (Review of Grades / Revaluation):** Students may appeal for grade review by paying a prescribed fee within the stipulated time; the fee is refunded if the grade is revised upward[cite: 17].
* **Clause 8.13 – 8.14 (SGPA & CGPA):** SGPA = $\sum(\text{Course Credits} \times \text{Grade Points}) / \sum(\text{Course Credits})$ for that semester excluding transitional grades[cite: 17]. CGPA calculates the same across all semesters excluding "F" and transitional grades[cite: 17].
* **Clause 8.15 (Awarding Class):**
  * **First Class with Distinction (FCD):** $\text{CGPA} \ge 7.0$ ($\ge 70\%$)[cite: 17]
  * **First Class (FC):** $6.0 \le \text{CGPA} < 7.0$ ($\ge 60\%$ and $< 70\%$)[cite: 17]
  * **Second Class (SC):** $5.0 \le \text{CGPA} < 6.0$ ($\ge 50\%$ and $< 60\%$)[cite: 17]
  * **Pass (PS):** $4.0 \le \text{CGPA} < 5.0$ ($\ge 40\%$ and $< 50\%$)[cite: 17]
* **Clause 8.16 – 8.17 (CGPA Make-Up Provision):** If a student does not achieve the minimum CGPA (5.00 in base regulations), they may reappear in SEE for theory courses of any semester without rejecting CIE marks (with written permission from the Controller of Examinations) within the maximum programme duration[cite: 17].
* **Clause 8.18 (CGPA to Percentage Formula):** $\text{Percentage of Marks} = \text{CGPA} \times 10$ (used only after successful completion of the programme)[cite: 17].

## 9. Examinations

* **Clause 9.1 – 9.2 (B.E. Passing Standards):**
  * **CIE Minimum:** $\ge 40\%$ (scoring $< 40\%$ results in **"NE" — Not Eligible** grade, updated to **"DX"** in 2025 Addendum)[cite: 17, 18, 25].
  * **SEE Minimum:** $\ge 35\%$[cite: 18].
  * **Overall Passing Score (CIE + SEE):** $\ge 40\%$[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.3 (B.Arch. Passing Standards):** CIE $\ge 50\%$, SEE $\ge 40\%$, Overall $\ge 50\%$[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.4 – 9.5 (Course Completion & Absenteeism):** A course is completed if the student earns a letter grade other than "F", "I", or "X"[cite: 18]. Being absent in SEE without an approved "I" grade results in an "F" grade[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.6 – 9.8 (Backlogs & Re-registration):** Students with an "F" grade carry forward their first-attempt CIE marks into backlog examinations[cite: 18]. Students with an "NE" (now "DX") grade due to attendance or CIE shortage must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester and earn both attendance and CIE before writing SEE[cite: 18, 25].
* **Clause 9.9 (Course Exemption After 4 Failed Attempts):** If a student fails a course *other than a Professional Core Course (PCC)* even after **four attempts**, it may be exempted and replaced (in consultation with the Proctor) by another course of equal credits from the Board of Studies pool[cite: 18]. Limited to **two courses** (one at a time) across the programme[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.10 (Examination Malpractice):** Malpractice during CIE or SEE is referred to the Malpractice Review Committee, which submits a written report to the Principal for penal action[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.11 (Make-Up Examination):** Available for students awarded **"I"** or **"X"** grades[cite: 18]. Not available for supplementary semesters, re-registered subjects, or practicals[cite: 18]. Passing via make-up exam disqualifies the student from Rank awards[cite: 18].
* **Clause 9.12 – 9.13 (Supplementary Examinations):** Per VTU notification (24 June 2024), supplementary exams are held after 2nd sem (for 1st/2nd sem 'F' grades), after 4th sem (for 3rd/4th sem 'F' grades), and after 6th sem (for 5th/6th sem 'F' grades)[cite: 18]. No supplementary semester exists for the 2023 batch onwards[cite: 18].

## 10. Attendance Requirement

* **Clause 10.1 (85% Minimum Attendance & 10% Condonation):** Each semester is considered as a unit, and a candidate must maintain a **minimum attendance of 85% in each subject**, with a provision for **condonation of up to 10% of the attendance by the Principal** (bringing effective threshold to 75%) for valid reasons such as **medical emergencies, participation in sports, cultural activities, seminars, workshops, and paper presentations** at University, State, or National levels.
* **Clause 10.2 (Attendance Calculation Period):** Calculated per the College's Calendar of Events (or from the date of admission for late admissions)[cite: 19].
* **Clause 10.3 (Attendance Shortage Penalty):** Failure to fulfill attendance requirements bars the student from attending SEE in that subject and results in an **"NE"** grade (updated to **"DX"** in the 2025 Addendum)[cite: 19, 25].

---

## 11. Rejection of Results

* **Clause 11.1 – 11.5 (One-Time Semester/Year Rejection):** A student may reject the total performance of a semester or year **only once** during the entire period of study (not permitted for 8th semester Project Work)[cite: 19]. Rejecting requires repeating that semester via readmission in the subsequent academic year while retaining the original USN and remaining within the overall maximum programme duration[cite: 19].

---

## 12. Temporary Break of Study / Readmission

* **Clause 12.1 – 12.4 (Temporary Withdrawal):** Permitted only for valid reasons (such as accident or hospitalization due to prolonged ill health) via advance application through the HOD to the Principal before the exam fee deadline, with supporting documents, parent/guardian endorsement, and zero pending college/library dues[cite: 19]. The break period counts toward the maximum programme duration[cite: 19].
* **Clause 12.5 – 12.8 (Detention & Readmission Rules):** Detention periods do not count as a "Break of Study"[cite: 19]. Re-admitted students must follow the prevailing scheme and are ineligible for Merit Ranks[cite: 19]. Students failing to obtain eligibility for the 3rd semester even after 3 academic years must discontinue or seek fresh readmission to 1st semester with a new USN[cite: 19].

---

## 13. Vertical Progression

* **Clause 13.1.1 – 13.1.2 (Odd to Even Semester):** No credit barrier exists to progress from an odd semester to the next even semester (unless detained or facing disciplinary action), provided the student satisfied CIE requirements and registered for SEE[cite: 19].
* **Clause 13.2.1 (First Year to Second Year Progression):** Students with **"F" grades totaling not more than 16 credits** across 1st and 2nd semesters are eligible to enter Second Year[cite: 19]. Non-credit mandatory courses (NCMC) are excluded from the 16-credit count[cite: 19].
* **Clause 13.2.2 (Second Year Onwards):** From 3rd semester onwards, students can progress to the next semester irrespective of the number of "F" or "NE" grades[cite: 19].
* **Clause 13.2.3 (Eligibility for 7th Semester B.E. / 9th Semester B.Arch.):** Students must have **zero "F" or "NE" (or "DX"/"AB") grades in First Year (1st and 2nd semesters)** to enter 7th semester B.E. or 9th semester B.Arch.[cite: 19, 25]
* **Clause 13.3 – 13.4 (Lateral Entry Mandatory Bridge Courses):** Polytechnic Diploma lateral entry students must complete *Additional Mathematics I & II*[cite: 19]; B.Sc. lateral entry students must complete *Computer Aided Engineering Drawing, Basics of Civil Engineering, and Mechanics*[cite: 20].
* **Clause 13.5 – 13.8 (Academic Standing & Progression):** Minimum expected CGPA is 5.0 at the end of each academic year (first-time failure triggers a warning)[cite: 20]. Students must undergo coursework of the immediate preceding lower semester before promotion[cite: 20].

## 14. Rules for Lateral Entry Students in B.E. Programmes

* **Clauses 14.1 – 14.5:** Lateral entry students enter directly in 3rd semester, must finish within **6 academic years**, earn **120 credits**, compulsorily pass the two Mathematics bridge courses, and follow regular batch coursework from 3rd semester onwards[cite: 20].

---

## 15. Award of a Rank Under Autonomous Scheme

* **Clauses 15.1 – 15.6 (Merit Rank Eligibility):** Declared only for students directly admitted to MSRIT who complete the entire programme within minimum prescribed duration (4 years B.E., 5 years B.Arch., 3 years Lateral Entry) and **pass all subjects in the first attempt only** (zero 'F', 'X', or 'I' grades and no result rejections)[cite: 20]. B.E. rank is computed on CGPA from **3rd to 8th semester** (3 ranks if intake $\le 60$; 4 ranks if intake $> 60$)[cite: 20].

---

## 16. Award of Honours and Minor Degree

* **Clause 16.0 (Mutual Exclusion):** No student is permitted to register for both Honours and Minor degrees simultaneously[cite: 20].
* **Clause 16.1 (Honours Degree):** Requires **18 or more additional credits** via institute-approved MOOCs (SWAYAM/NPTEL, 1 credit per 4 weeks), starting from **5th semester**, requiring **$\text{CGPA} \ge 7.50$ at the end of 4th semester** and a minimum final score of **60%** in each coursework[cite: 20].
* **Clause 16.2 (Minor Degree):** Starts from **4th semester**, requires **$\text{CGPA} > 5.0$ after 3rd semester**, and requires earning **18 additional credits** across semesters 4–8 with $\text{CGPA} \ge 5.00$ before completing the major degree[cite: 20, 21].

## 17. Activity Points (AICTE Mandatory)

* **Clause 17 (Mandatory AICTE Activity Points):** Non-credit mandatory points recorded on the 8th semester grade card[cite: 21]:
  * **Regular 4-year B.E. students:** 100 Activity Points[cite: 21]
  * **Lateral Entry (2nd year) students:** 75 Activity Points[cite: 21]

## 18. Termination from the Programme

* **Clauses 18.1 – 18.3:** Admission may be terminated if a student: (1) fails to complete requirements within the maximum stipulated duration, (2) violates institute discipline norms, or (3) **remains absent from classes for more than six weeks at a time in a semester without granted leave of absence**[cite: 21].

---

## 19. Conduct and Discipline

* **Clauses 19.1 – 19.9:** Students must maintain decorum inside and outside campus[cite: 21]. Willful property damage, exam malpractice, or unruly behavior invites penalties ranging from fines and probation to withdrawal of placement services, withholding of degrees, or expulsion[cite: 21]. **Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate expulsion[cite: 21].** **No attendance is granted during a suspension period[cite: 21].**

## 21. Additional Regulations / Guidelines 2024 for Autonomous Institutions (VTU Superseding Addendum — Dated 20.02.2025)

**Authority:** As per VTU Directions to Autonomous College Principals on 25.02.2025 (Notification No. `VTU/PS/2024-25/223` dated 20.02.2025)[cite: 24]. Applicable from Academic Year 2024–25 for UG & PG programmes[cite: 24]. **Supersedes all previous Guidelines / Norms issued by the University[cite: 24].**

* **Addendum Clause 21.1 (Updated Semester Structure):**
  * **Odd Semester:** 20 Weeks[cite: 24]
  * **Even Semester:** 20 Weeks[cite: 24]
  * **Summer Semester:** 10 Weeks (8 weeks classes + 2 weeks exams; **UG programmes only**, not applicable to PG)[cite: 24, 25].
* **Addendum Clause 21.2 (Updated Letter Grades — Introduction of 'DX', 'AB', 'W'):**
  * **'DX':** Shortage of Attendance or Shortage of CIE Marks or both in a credit course (replaces legacy 'NE' grade)[cite: 24, 25].
  * **'NP':** Not passed in a Non-credit course[cite: 24].
  * **'PP':** Passed in a Non-credit course[cite: 24].
  * **'AB':** Absent for a course in SEE[cite: 24].
  * **'AU':** Satisfactory performance in an audit course[cite: 24].
  * **'F':** Fail in a course in SEE[cite: 24].
  * **'I' / 'X':** Transitional Grades (eligible for Make-up Exams in Autonomous Colleges)[cite: 24].
  * **'W':** Transitional Grade — Withdrawal from course[cite: 24].
* **Addendum Clause 21.3 (Summer Semester Rules — UG Only):**
  * Open for clearing **'DX' / 'NP'** grade courses, dropped/withdrawn courses, backlog **'F' / 'AB'** courses, and for **extraordinary students ($\text{CGPA} \ge 9.0$)** wishing to register for higher semester courses to complete the B.E. programme in **3.5 years**[cite: 25].
  * Slow learners may drop courses in a regular semester and take them in the Summer Semester[cite: 25].
  * **Maximum Summer Registration:** 14 credits for UG Engineering; 18 credits for B.Arch.[cite: 25] Project work and internships are **not permitted** in Summer Semester[cite: 25]. No make-up exams exist for Summer Semester[cite: 24].
* **Addendum Clause 21.4 (Updated Course Registration Floor):**
  * **Minimum:** 16 credits | **Maximum:** 28 credits per regular semester[cite: 25]. Dropping/withdrawing from a course cannot reduce total registered credits below 16[cite: 25].
* **Addendum Clause 21.5 (Updated Vertical Progression Rules - UG):**
  * **To II Year:** 'F' grades totaling not more than 16 credits across I & II Semesters[cite: 25].
  * **To III Year:** No restrictions (any number of backlogs allowed)[cite: 25].
  * **To IV Year (7th Semester):** Zero `'F'`, `'DX'`, or `'AB'` grades permitted in any I & II Semester courses[cite: 25].
* **Addendum Clause 21.6 (PG Programme Rules):** No Summer Semester for PG; `'DX'`/`'NP'` courses must be re-registered in subsequent Odd/Even semesters without timetable clashes[cite: 26].
* **Addendum Clause 21.7 (Updated Minimum CGPA for Degree Award):**
  * **UG Programmes:** Minimum **$\text{CGPA} \ge 4.00$** (supersedes earlier 5.00 requirement)[cite: 15, 26].
  * **PG Programmes:** Minimum **$\text{CGPA} \ge 5.00$**[cite: 26].
  * Must also earn all required credits, hold zero transitional grades, pass all MNC/Audit courses, and earn prescribed AICTE Activity Points[cite: 26].
* **Addendum Clause 21.8 (Award of Ranks):** Declared at Institute level for students passing all prescribed courses and credits in the **first attempt** across Odd, Even, and Summer Semesters (every SEE appearance or absence counts as an attempt)[cite: 26].
* **Addendum Clause 21.9 (Successive Failures):** If a student fails a non-core course after **4 attempts**, an equivalent alternative course recommended by the Board of Studies (BOS) may be opted for (maximum **2 courses** allowed)[cite: 26].
